Player Feedback

Good games improve through testing and feedback. Share your thoughts about the driving experience, city environment, navigation, and future features to help shape the development of Dash N Dine.

What to share

Help Improve Dash N Dine

Useful feedback can identify what feels good, what is confusing, and what needs more work. Whether you found a bug or have an idea for a new feature, your observations can help guide future updates.

  • Was driving easy to understand?
  • Did navigating the city feel natural?
  • Did you encounter any bugs or glitches?
  • Did the vehicle selection work as expected?
  • What feature would you like to see next?
When reporting a bug, describe what you were doing, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. This makes the issue easier to reproduce and investigate.
