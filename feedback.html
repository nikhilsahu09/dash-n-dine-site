Turning feedback into updates

How playtest feedback can guide design decisions.

Feedback process
01

Review driving and controls

Use player ratings and written comments to identify handling or navigation changes worth testing.

02

Evaluate the delivery loop

Look for patterns in comments about finding destinations, completing deliveries, and understanding objectives.

03

Prioritize using real responses

Combine rating trends, reported bugs, and written feedback to decide which changes should come first.
