Player Feedback

Good games improve through testing and feedback. This page is intended to collect thoughts about the driving experience, city environment, and future features.

What to share

Help improve Dash N Dine

Useful feedback can help identify what feels good, what is confusing, and what needs more work.

  • Was driving easy to understand?
  • Did navigating the city feel natural?
  • Did you encounter any bugs?
  • What feature would you like to see next?
Be specific when describing a bug. Include what you were doing and what you expected to happen.
