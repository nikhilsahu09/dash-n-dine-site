Feedback is most useful when it's specific. If you've tried the game in Godot or reviewed the screenshots, comments on controls, navigation, the city layout, or confusing behavior can help prioritize the next round of work.

  • What felt clear or confusing?
  • Did a route or waypoint seem wrong?
  • Did the garage select the vehicle you expected?
  • What would you improve first?
If you're reporting a bug, include what you tried, what you expected, and what happened instead. The more specific the report, the easier it is to investigate.
