The idea behind the game

About the Project.

Dash N Dine is an independent 3D driving game project. I'm building the city and the systems that let a vehicle move through it, follow routes, and connect the different parts of the gameplay.

The idea

A city, a vehicle, and a route to follow

Dash N Dine started as a game-development project centered on driving through a 3D city. The work is as much about making the systems function together as it is about building the environment players see.

01 / WORLD

Designing the city

Roads, sidewalks, blocks, and buildings give the game its layout. Their placement matters because the environment has to be readable and usable from behind the wheel.

02 / NAVIGATION

Connecting the roads

The waypoint network represents paths through the city. The challenge is connecting markers in a way that respects the roads and can guide a route toward the correct destination.

03 / GAMEPLAY

Getting into the right vehicle

The garage and driving scene need to agree about which vehicle the player selected. This makes scene transitions and the data passed between systems important parts of the implementation.

04 / PROCESS

Learning through debugging

When something behaves differently than expected, I trace it through the scene setup, scripts, and project settings, then test a focused change rather than guessing at a fix.

Tools & approach

Built in Godot

Godot and GDScript are the foundation of the project. Development includes 3D scene construction, gameplay scripting, waypoint logic, and testing both in the editor and in exported builds.
