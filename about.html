The idea behind the game

About the Project.

Dine N Dash is an independent 3D driving and delivery game built around navigating a city, selecting vehicles, and following routes to destinations. The project brings together environmental design, vehicle systems, and navigation logic in an interactive driving experience.

The concept

A city, a vehicle, and a route to follow

Dine N Dash combines the freedom of city driving with the structure of destination-based gameplay. Its design connects the environment, navigation network, and vehicle selection into a unified game experience.

01 / WORLD

Designing the city

Roads, sidewalks, blocks, and buildings form a navigable 3D environment. The layout provides the structure for driving, exploring the city, and reaching destinations.

02 / NAVIGATION

Connecting the roads

A waypoint network represents paths through the city and supports routefinding between locations. Its structure connects destinations to the surrounding road network.

03 / GAMEPLAY

Vehicle selection

The garage and driving systems connect vehicle selection with gameplay. Scene transitions and shared data help maintain the selected vehicle as the player moves into the driving experience.

04 / ENGINEERING

Building reliable systems

The project combines scene organization, GDScript, navigation logic, and systematic debugging. Testing and iteration help identify issues and improve how individual systems work together.

Tools & technology

Built in Godot

Godot Engine and GDScript power the project's gameplay and interactive 3D environment. The technical implementation brings together scene construction, vehicle systems, waypoint routing, resource management, and debugging.
