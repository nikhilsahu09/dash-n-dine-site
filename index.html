The project

More than a drive around the block

The goal is to connect the parts of the game into one coherent experience: a city to move through, vehicles to drive, and routes that lead to the right destination.

▦

A city built piece by piece

Roads, sidewalks, buildings, and blocks form the environment the driving systems have to work within.
