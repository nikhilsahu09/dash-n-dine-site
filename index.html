01 / Overview

From an idea to a playable world.

Dash N Dine is an independent game development project built in Godot. The project explores how a 3D driving environment can connect roads, destinations, and navigation into a cohesive gameplay experience.

This portfolio documents the design decisions, technical challenges, player testing, and improvements behind the game.

02 / Engineering

Building the systems behind the drive.

The project involves more than creating a city to drive through. Its systems must work together to make the world navigable and the experience playable.

City Environment

Developing a 3D urban environment with roads, sidewalks, blocks, and buildings to create a connected driving world.

Road Network

Connecting waypoints to represent road paths and help the game determine routes through the environment.

Navigation

Working on route selection and destination-based navigation so the player can reach intended locations.

Debugging & Iteration

Testing the game, diagnosing unexpected behavior, and refining systems as development progresses.
