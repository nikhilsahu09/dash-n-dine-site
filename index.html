The Concept

Driving Meets Delivery

A game project combining a 3D city environment with vehicle movement and route navigation.

Dash N Dine is a driving game project focused on creating a navigable city and connecting its different gameplay systems. The goal is to build an experience where moving through the environment and reaching destinations are central to gameplay.

Developed with Godot, the project brings together 3D scene building, vehicle selection, road networks, and waypoint-based navigation. It is also an opportunity to work through the practical challenges of building, testing, and exporting a game.
